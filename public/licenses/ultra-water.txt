Forge water material attribution

Adapted from Humpalumps/Ultra-Water at commit
ce9248fa20afbdf63fd4c11709f68d7368aca0a0:
src/water/bakeTextures.js (offline procedural texture generation),
src/water/OceanSurface.js (foam erosion and billow lighting, environment/planar
coverage composition, roughness-aware Fresnel, GGX sun and facet sparkle).
Forge changes: offline CPU bake, shared texture lifetime, existing Lake/River
contact masks, authored scale/flow, Forge lighting/shadows and alpha composition.
Reflection changes: reuse Forge PMREM and shared planar targets, transparent
geometry capture, coverage-aware fallback, existing quality ceilings and Off.
Sun changes: perceptual roughness converted to GGX alpha, finite peak footprint,
Forge linear-light scaling, filtered river/lake normals, bounded two-scale hash
facets in place of the demo's Poisson counts, distance fade and horizon gating.

The water solar-disc atmospheric extinction in skyAtmosphere.ts additionally
adapts coefficients and equations from the installed three.js Sky.js shader.
